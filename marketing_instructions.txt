[0] Marketing Campaign & Multi-Channel ROI Analytics Platform
[1] 1. Project Overview
[2] Marketing companies invest millions of rupees in digital advertising across multiple platforms such as Google Ads, Meta (Facebook & Instagram) Ads, and Email Marketing. However, each platform provides its own reports, making it difficult to understand overall campaign performance.
[3] This project builds a centralized analytics platform that collects, cleans, stores, analyzes, and visualizes marketing data from multiple channels. It helps businesses identify which campaigns are performing well, optimize marketing budgets, and improve return on investment (ROI).
[4] 2. Problem Statement
[5] Businesses face several challenges:
[6] Marketing data is spread across multiple platforms. 
[7] Reports use different formats and metrics. 
[8] It is difficult to compare campaign performance. 
[9] Marketing teams cannot accurately identify the best-performing channel. 
[10] Customer acquisition cost is often unknown. 
[11] Decisions are based on incomplete or manual analysis. 
[12] This project solves these problems by integrating all marketing data into a single analytics platform.
